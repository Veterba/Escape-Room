# Credits

## Character sprites

The character sprites (the hero, the Ammo Wraith and all the Kin enemies, files 01–08 in
`art/source/`) are **placeholders** taken from a community Enter the Gungeon sprite sheet.
They are **not for public release** and will be replaced with our own art.

## Other art

Guns and items (files 09–21) were drawn for the project mood board. Tiles, crates, barrels, chains,
bullets, shell casings, the muzzle flash, the hand and UI frames are drawn in code in `tools/draw_art.py`.
