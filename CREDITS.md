# Credits

## Character sprites

The character sprites of the Grunt Kin and the Fuse Kin (files 03 and 05 in `art/source/`) are **placeholders** taken from a community Enter the Gungeon sprite sheet. They are **not for
public release** and will be replaced with our own art.

Root, the boss (`art/source/retexture/44_boss_root/`), is the owner's approved boss design; its
eye, glow and effects are drawn in code from the approved page.

The Watcher, Ram, Glitch and Trojan (`art/source/retexture/37_enemies_sheet.png`) are the owner's
approved retexture enemies.

The hero, Telly (`art/source/retexture/17_hero_sheet.png`), and the starting gun
(`18_gun_starter.png`) are the approved retexture designs. Telly copies a reference robot sprite
pixel for pixel (adding an antenna and a chest LED); check where that reference comes from before
any public release.

## Other art

Guns and items (files 09–21) were drawn for the project mood board; they are now only the HUD's gun
icons and the heart, ammo, smoke bomb and hourglass items. The guns in the hand, the chests and the
key are the owner's approved retexture sprites (`art/source/retexture/`), and so are the room tiles and
objects (`art/source/retexture/38_room_parts/`). The rooms' floor details, the Boss Chamber's pylons, barrels, doors, the exit hatch, the key's glint,
bullets, shell casings, the muzzle flash, the hero's hand and UI frames are drawn in code in `tools/draw_art.py`.
